# Mascots

Each app has a mascot with a name borrowed from a word in another language.

| App | Mascot | Word |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`hijaiyah/`](hijaiyah) | Sada | Arabic, "echo" |
| [`aksara/`](aksara) | Jola | Icelandic, from *Jólabókaflóð* |
| [`logica/`](logica) | Poka | Japanese, from *poka-yoke* |
| [`lexica/`](lexica) | Pochi | Russian, from *pochemuchka* |
| [`numeria/`](numeria) | Ollie | the owl |

## Sada (Hijaiyah)

Sada comes from the Arabic word *ṣadā* (صدى), meaning "echo". Just like an echo, Sada repeats every letter sound back to you, so you can listen, say it, and hear it again until it feels easy.

## Jola (Aksara)

Jola is short for *Jólabókaflóð*, the Icelandic "Christmas book flood". On Christmas Eve, Icelandic families give each other books and spend the evening reading together. *Jóla* is the first part of that word, from *jól* ("Yule", Christmas). Jola brings the same cosy feeling of a good book and a good story.

## Poka (Logica)

Poka is short for *poka-yoke* (ポカヨケ), a Japanese idea meaning "mistake-proofing": designing things so that a mistake is noticed and fixed before it causes trouble. Poka the robot does the same. It follows your program step by step, so you can see where a mistake hides, and then fix it.

## Pochi (Lexica)

Pochi is short for *pochemuchka* (почемучка), a Russian word for a person who keeps asking "why?" (*почему*). Pochi is curious about every word: what it means, how it sounds, and where it comes from.

## Ollie (Numeria)

Ollie is a friendly owl who helps with everyday math, from counting apples in the kitchen to working out discounts in the shop.

Only Poka and Ollie are named on screen so far. The mascots of the other apps are described here for when they are named in the apps.
